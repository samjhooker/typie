# Competitor Audit: WisprFlow + Superwhisper

*Sources: live DOM extraction of wisprflow.ai and superwhisper.com (copy, structure, story arc). Screenshots pending from Sam for the typography/motion layer. Question on the table: how does typie become radical AND professional at the same time?*

---

## The headline finding

**Neither competitor wins on product truth. Both win on narrative discipline.**

- WisprFlow's entire site speaks one verb: *flow*. "Start flowing." "Effortless." "Download for free" appears within seconds. The brand is a verb you perform.
- Superwhisper's page is built around a single big idea — **"Adaptability"** — with a human truth under it ("Communication isn't one-size-fits-all"). Every feature becomes a chapter of that thesis instead of a list item.

Typie already has its thesis. It's sitting right there in the H1: **"It types what you say. Then it keeps going."** What's missing isn't a story — it's committing to telling *only that story* for the full scroll length.

---

## WisprFlow — what makes it beautiful

1. **The comparison page is the killer asset.** "Why Flow vs. Built-in Dictation" with side-by-side real examples. Evidence reads as confidence. They're not afraid of the incumbent — they invite the fight.
2. **Persona ladder, kept off the homepage.** Leaders, Developers, Creators, Students, Lawyers, Sales, Accessibility — each a separate page. Homepage stays pure; depth lives one click away.
3. **Disarming honesty about the free tier.** "Free, with no trial countdown or credit card required, for 2,000 words per week." Saying the *limit* plainly builds more trust than hiding it.
4. **Practical care content.** A microphone guide; a caveat that AirPods hurt accuracy. Telling people what NOT to expect is a credibility superpower.
5. **Restraint in the ask.** One primary CTA repeated quietly. No competing links in the hero.

## Superwhisper — what makes it polished

1. **Benefit-first feature headings with personality.** "Duh, yes online too — Works offline." The benefit is the headline; the feature is the sub-line. Personality without noise.
2. **Depth as theater.** The simulated settings UI — model lists (GPT-5, Whisper Large), presets, per-app activation — is a *prop*. It says "serious tool for serious people" without a word of claim.
3. **Named social proof.** "Used by those who move fast" + Vercel, Eureka Labs, @perplexity_ai. Specific beats generic every time.
4. **A professional trust stack.** SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, 30-day refund, roadmap, docs, video tutorials *with durations*.
5. **Tools as marketing.** A free mic test, a free voice recorder. Utility pages that rank, help, and convert.

## What typie has that neither can match

1. **Actually free, actually open source.** WisprFlow rations 2,000 words/week. Superwhisper gates Pro behind toggles. Typie can say: *no word counts, no account, no upgrade nudge — the pricing page is a 404.* **Neither competitor can tell this story. It's typie's unfair advantage and it's currently buried.**
2. **Privacy as physics, not paperwork.** Superwhisper needs SOC 2 + HIPAA because audio touches servers. Typie's network path doesn't exist. Superwhisper buys certificates; typie ships `lsof -p $(pgrep typie) -i` returning empty. That's not better compliance — it's a different *category* of trust.
3. **Call capture.** Neither leads with it. It's a second product moment competitors don't have.
4. **The live Mac replay.** Both competitors use videos. Typie's demo is a real, interactive scene machine sitting in the page. It should be the most famous demo in the category.

## Where typie currently loses

- **Their pages tell a story with chapters; ours is a museum of well-spaced rooms.** Beautiful rooms, but no hallway connecting them. Flow: speak → typed → *kept safe* → yours. Each section should hand off to the next.
- **Superwhisper shows depth as theater (settings prop); we hide our depth inside a demo most people won't click.** The 80ms/Parakeet/25-languages engineering story is invisible.
- **We never name the enemy.** Both competitors win by naming the incumbent (built-in dictation). Typie never says who it's replacing.
- **"Used by" is empty for us right now** — fabricating logos would be worse than none. Our equivalent: the open-source repo itself. Stars, commits, the audit trail — *show the receipt.*

---

## The verdict

Don't become them. **Take their discipline, keep our nerve.** The radical-professional play for typie is to be the only dictation site on the internet that behaves like its product: instant, honest, and provably offline. Everyone else has a privacy *page*. Typie can have privacy as a *mechanic*.
