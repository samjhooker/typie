# Typie Homepage Audit — the blunt one

*Date: audit of the current landing page. References: interfere.png (monochrome restraint) + abode.png (playful but disciplined). Verdict framework: what's important, what's worth it, what wows, what nobody reads.*

---

## The one-line verdict

**The Mac demo is the product. Everything else on this page is competing with it, and losing — loudly.**

interfere and abode both share a skeleton: one headline, one support line, one ask, one hero visual, then air. Typie's first screen currently has **13 competing elements** before you even scroll. That's not powerful. That's anxious.

## Where the same guarantee appears (it's six)

"100% free / offline / no account / open source" shows up in:

1. Hero eyebrow pill
2. Hero badges row
3. The strip section
4. Security section cards
5. FAQ answers
6. Final CTA sub-line

Six tellings of the same thing = zero tellings. Once, said with confidence, is worth more than six said nervously.

## What wows

- **The Mac demo.** Real app UIs, real keystrokes landing in Slack/Docs, the notch island that behaves like the actual product. This is the best asset on the page and it deserves to be the *only* show in town on screen one.
- The notch island fidelity (now matching the real app screenshots). Keep.
- The transcript detail view in the transcribe scene. Keep.

## What's worth it (keep, but quiet down)

- **H1** — "It types what you say." is genuinely good. The second line + italic serif flourish is decoration; consider keeping just the first line as the statement.
- **Works-everywhere marquee** — one row of app icons says "any app" faster than any paragraph. Keep one row, not three.
- **Security section** — it's the differentiator. But it's built like a swap-meet: 3 cards + expandable terminal proof + repeated guarantees. interferes's security section is two quiet cards. Ours should be 2–3 numbered rows, proof tucked away for skeptics.
- **FAQ** — people who are about to install read FAQs. Keep, but 8 items in a 2-col grid reads like legal. 6 items, single column, quieter.
- **Final CTA** — fine idea, over-decorated (gradient text, sparkle pseudo-elements).

## What nobody reads (cut or demote)

- **Sub paragraphs over 2 lines** — every section has one. Cut each to ≤1 line.
- **Testimonial grid** — 3 cards with full names, cities, tenure ("6 months daily"). Feels fabricated, and fabricated-feeing trust signals cost more than they earn. One strong quote, or none.
- **The strip section** — pure duplication. Delete the whole section.
- **Guarantee badges + eyebrow** — pick zero or one, not both.
- **Stage-head / hint text** — the demo should invite exploration by being irresistible, not by instructions.

## The prescribe (minimal, powerful)

interfere's hero is: tiny logo, small nav, **H1 + one line + one button**, then a giant product screenshot that IS the page. abode's hero is: one playful visual, **two-line headline, one line, one pill button**. Neither has an eyebrow pill, a badge row, four tab-cards, and two CTAs.

**Hero v3:**

1. H1 (one line if we're brave), one ≤20-word sub, **one black CTA** + quiet GitHub text link
2. Four feature words as **minimal text tabs** (Dictate · Capture · Transcribe · Notes) driving the Mac scenes — the existing demo engine is untouched
3. The Mac, full-width beneath, running its auto-tour. Nothing else on screen one.

Then the rest of the page gets quieter in task order (see homepage-tasks.md). One accent color. One voice. One ask.
